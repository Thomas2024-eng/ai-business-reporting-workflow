# ai-business-reporting-workflow
Exploring practical applications of AI, data analytics and workflow automation in business reporting.
In my work in business analytics and sales performance at Standard Chartered Bank Kenya, I have seen how important reliable reporting is to good decision-making. I have also seen how much time can go into preparing reports, checking figures, tracking performance, and turning raw data into information that business teams can act on.
That experience has made me curious about how AI and automation could make these processes simpler, faster, and more consistent.
I have been exploring how skills in Python, SQL, statistics, and machine learning can be applied to everyday business challenges. One idea I am particularly interested in is a Business Reporting Assistant that could help analysts validate sales data, calculate key performance indicators, identify unusual results, and prepare a first draft of a management report.
The idea is to bring several routine reporting tasks into one structured workflow. Rather than spending most of their time preparing figures, analysts could spend more time investigating trends, understanding what is driving performance, and helping decision-makers determine what to do next.
I also recognize that automation is not a substitute for good judgement. Data needs to be checked, unexpected results need to be investigated, and AI-generated summaries need to be reviewed before they are shared. I am interested in understanding how to build these checks into a workflow so that efficiency does not come at the expense of accuracy or accountability.
I am currently developing my technical skills through the AFRICDSA Certified Data Scientist program, where I am learning Python, statistics, machine learning, deep learning, SQL, and data visualization. I want to put these skills into practice by working on projects that address real business and operational problems.
Process Street interests me because of its focus on workflows, process management, and automation. I see an opportunity to explore how structured workflows and AI can work together to reduce repetitive work, improve consistency, and give teams more time to focus on meaningful tasks.
My goal is to keep learning by building practical solutions, testing ideas, and understanding what it takes to make automation useful in a real working environment.
